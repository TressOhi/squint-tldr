Squint

Squint auto-rejects cookie banners and flags the risky parts of privacy policies and terms.

Anthropic API key

Policy scans use Claude through your own key. You pay Anthropic directly, usually a few cents per policy. Cookie auto-reject doesn't need a key.

No key yet? Create one at platform.claude.com. Setting a monthly spend limit there is a good idea.

Paused sites

    None. Pause a site from the Squint side panel.

    Privacy

    • Your key is stored only in this browser profile, in the extension's local storage.
    • When you scan, the document's text goes straight from your browser to Anthropic's API. There is no Squint server.
    • Cookie auto-reject runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
    • Recent scan results are kept locally so reopening a policy doesn't cost another scan.
