    Privacy

    • Your keys are stored only in this browser profile, in the extension's local storage.
    • When you scan, the document's text goes straight from your browser to the provider you chose. There is no Squint server. With a provider that runs on your computer, nothing leaves it.
    • Cookie auto-reject runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
    • Recent scan results are kept locally so reopening a policy doesn't cost another scan.
